SEPHIRIA - Fonts

These third-party fonts are used in SEPHIRIA. They are NOT included in
this kit; each keeps its own license. Download them from the sources
below. (Some may be fan fonts -- verify the license before redistributing.)

  - Galmuri (Galmuri11)
      Used for: EN / KO / ES / PT UI
      License: SIL Open Font License 1.1 [verified]
      Source: https://github.com/quiple/galmuri

  - GalCyrillic (Galmuri Cyrillic)
      Used for: RU UI
      License: Project-internal TMP SDF font (no standalone .ttf); a Cyrillic build derived from Galmuri, so inherits Galmuri's SIL OFL 1.1. No separate public distribution -- confirm the RU-build author.
      Source: https://galmuri.quiple.dev (base font)

  - JF Dot M+ 10 (JF-Dot-MPlus10)
      Used for: JA UI
      License: Jikasei dot font: copyright waived, free incl. commercial & redistribution (based on M+ BITMAP FONTS) [verified]
      Source: http://jikasei.me/font/jf-dotfont/

  - Fusion Pixel Font 10px
      Used for: ZH UI
      License: SIL OFL 1.1, reserved name 'Fusion Pixel' [verified]
      Source: https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font
