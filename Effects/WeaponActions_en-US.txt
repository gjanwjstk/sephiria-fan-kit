======================================
  Weapon_SwordAndShield_1
======================================
[Actions]
  ■ Normal Attack
    Input: Left Click (3 times)
  ■ Dash attack
    Input: (After dashing) Left Click
  ■ Block
    Input: Right Click (hold)
  ■ Special Attack: Cleave
    Input: While guarding Left Click

[Tutorial text]
  Block: Right Click Hold
  Block allows you to ward off all enemy attacks.
  Block in the direction of the enemy's attack. Blocking consumes 10 MP.
  If you use Block with precise timing, Perfect guard activates and consumes half the MP.
  When your MP reaches 0, your guard is dropped and you cannot block attacks.
  Perfect guard: block at the right time
  Cleave: While blocking Left Click
  Using Cleave expends MP.
  Successfully guarding reduces the cost of Cleave by 50%, and guarding perfectly reduces it by 100%.

======================================
  Weapon_GreatSword_1
======================================
[Actions]
  ■ Normal Attack
    Input: Left Click (3 times)
  ■ Dash attack
    Input: After dashing Left Click
  ■ Special Attack: Whirlwind
    Input: Right Click charge, then release

[Tutorial text]
  You cannot block attacks with a greatsword, but its powerful ATK and range make up for it.
  Use MP to perform a powerful Whirlwind attack over a large area.
  The Rage Gauge can be filled using the following actions.
  - Charging
- Attacking enemies
- Taking damage
  Charge: (hold) Right Click
  Whirlwind: (Release) Right Click

======================================
  Weapon_Dagger_1
======================================
[Actions]
  ■ Normal Attack
    Input: Left Click (5 times)
  ■ Dash attack
    Input: (After dashing) Left Click
  ■ Special Attack: Parry
    Input: Right Click
    Description: On successful Special Attack: Parry, gain Trance.
  ■ Special Attack: Fury
    Input: Trance Buff Active Right Click

[Tutorial text]
  The dagger is a weapon with a narrow range and low stagger, but it allows for swift attacks.
  Special Attack: Parry allows you to deflect enemy attacks by consuming MP.
  Special Attack: Parry will only deflect enemy attacks while you are glowing blue.
  If you successfully deflect an attack with Special Attack: Parry, you gain the Trance buff.
  While the Trance buff is active, you can use Special Attack: Fury.
  Special Attack: Fury:
  Special Attack: Fury: In Trance state Right Click
  Special Attack: Parry: Right Click

======================================
  Weapon_Crossbow
======================================
[Actions]
  ■ Normal Attack
    Input: Left Click
    Description: Automatically reloads when all arrows are exhausted. Move Speed is reduced by 40% during attacks.
  ■ Dash attack
    Input: (After dashing) Left Click
    Description: Consumes a dash to deploy a mine that lasts for 3 seconds. It automatically explodes when an enemy is detected.
  ■ Special Attack: Power Arrow
    Input: Right Click
    Description: Consume 10 MP to fire a powerful arrow. Does not consume arrows.

[Tutorial text]
  The crossbow is a ranged weapon. It attacks by consuming arrows in the magazine.
  When the magazine is completely depleted, you can refill it through Reload.
  Weapon Specialization Action (Reload): G
  When a Special Attack is cast, it fires an 'enhanced bullet' that pierces all enemies.
  Install small mines nearby through Dash attack. (Consumes dash gauge) They explode when enemies get close.

======================================
  Weapon_Staff
======================================
[Actions]
  ■ Normal Attack
    Input: Left Click (4 times)
  ■ Dash attack
    Input: (After dashing) Left Click
  ■ Special Attack: Riposte
    Input: Right Click

[Tutorial text]
  Staff is a weapon that can attack and defend at the same time by using Special Attack: Riposte, which is available even during Normal Attack.
  If you perform a Perfect guard against an enemy attack during Special Attack: Riposte, the second strike of this Special Attack is empowered.
  Special Attack: Riposte: Right Click

======================================
  Weapon_Katana
======================================
[Actions]
  ■ Normal Attack
    Input: Left Click (3 times)
  ■ Dash attack
    Input: (After dashing) Left Click
  ■ Sheath and Draw
    Input: Right Click
  ■ Special Attack: Heavy Slash
    Input: While Sheathed Left Click

[Tutorial text]
  Blade is a melee weapon that can alternate between Sheath and Unsheath states.
  In the Sheath state, you can use Special Attack: Heavy Slash by consuming 5 MP.
  You can block enemy attacks during sheathing and unsheathing.
  Unsheath: While in Sheath state Right Click
  Special Attack: Heavy Slash: While in Sheath state Left Click
  Sheath: While in Unsheath state Right Click
