Hard mode fate shards - 17 / language: en-US
================================================================================

=== Demon (Demon) ===
  Ferocious Claws (Lv 1-7)
    Increases enemy damage by 8/16/24/32/40/48/56%.
  Tenacious Body (Lv 1-7)
    Increases enemy HP by 15/25/35/45/55/65/75%.
  Protective Armor (Lv 1-3)
    As stages progress, enemies' Defense increases by 2/4/6.
  Agile Claws (Lv 1-2)
    Increases enemy Move Speed and ATKSPD by 20/35% during combat.
  Evil Energy (Lv 1-1)
    Damage taken by enemies in Super Armor state is reduced from 65% to 50%.
  Combat Stance (Lv 1-1)
    All bosses attack more aggressively.
  Festival of Blood (Lv 1-1)
    Enemies recover 15% HP when they attack the player. Mini Boss and Boss recover 6%.
  False Vanguard (Lv 1-1)
    Mini bosses become stronger.

=== World (World) ===
  Decline (Lv 1-3)
    HP recovery effects are reduced by 5/10/20%.
  Severed Exchange (Lv 1-2)
    Increases merchant Barter by 20/40.
  Rebellion of Machines (Lv 1-2)
    Increases trap damage by 100/200%.
  Proliferation (Lv 1-2)
    The number of regular enemies appearing increases by 10/25%.

=== Plan (Plan) ===
  Dispossession (Lv 1-2)
    The grade of tablets dropped by the boss is fixed to Advanced/Common.
  Dizziness (Lv 1-2)
    When selecting an artifact or tablet, the number of choices is reduced by 1/2/3.
  Deterioration (Lv 1-4)
    HP Restored on Level Up attributes decrease by 10/25/40/60%.
  Tension (Lv 1-1)
    Cannot use Potions during combat with Boss.
  Heavy Heart (Lv 1-1)
    You will start the exploration with 'Heavy Heart', an artifact with no abilities that cannot be discarded.
